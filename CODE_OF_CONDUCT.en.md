# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and friendly environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation and gender identity.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, friendly, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

**Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the whole community

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

**One additional item for this project:**

This project's methodology rests on **measured data**, so we care about one thing in particular: **do not alter numbers to make an argument look better.**

- When you cite data, give the source; do not retell it from impression.
- If you think our conclusions are wrong, **you are welcome to argue against them, but please bring data and reproduction steps**. Overturning our results is a valid contribution, not an offense.
- Do not present your own speculation as a measured result. **When you cannot tell the difference, say that it is only speculation.**

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards above, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, Issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reasons when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public. Examples of representing this project include: using an official email address, speaking through an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

---

## Reporting

If you encounter abuse, harassment or other unacceptable behavior, please report it through:

**Contact the maintainer privately via GitHub: [@luluxiuc](https://github.com/luluxiuc)**

All complaints will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obliged to respect the privacy and safety of the reporter.

**Do not report behavior problems through a public Issue.**

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will use the following levels to determine the consequences of a violation:

**1. Correction**

**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language, or other behavior considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private written warning from the maintainer, clarifying the nature of the violation and explaining why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested. If data fabrication is involved, a correction of the relevant statements is required.

**2. Warning**

**Community Impact:** A violation caused by a single incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning, with the consequences of continuing the behavior explained. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period, including unsolicited contact with those enforcing this Code of Conduct. Violating this will lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

**3. Temporary Ban**

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A ban on any form of interaction with the community or public communication for a specified period. Violating this will lead to a permanent ban.

**4. Permanent Ban**

**Community Impact:** Showing a pattern of violating community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassing an individual, or attacking or disparaging a class of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban on any form of public interaction within the project.
